Beef Share Pricing

Our cattle are responsibly raised on open Northern California pasture with a focus on grass-fed quality and sustainable ranching practices.

Package Estimated Take Home Weight Estimated Total Price
Quarter Beef 60–120 lbs $1,600–$2,000
Half Beef 180–250 lbs $3,000–$4,000

Pricing estimates include professional processing and custom cut/wrap options. Final pricing may vary depending on hanging weight and customer processing selections.

Beef is processed locally through Bud’s Custom Meats in Sonoma County.
