Grass-Fed Northern California Beef

Middletown Cattle Company raises grass-fed cattle on open Northern California pasture with a focus on quality, sustainability, and responsible ranching practices.

Our cattle are raised with an emphasis on natural grazing, herd health, and premium beef quality.

Processing

Beef is professionally processed and custom cut through Bud’s Custom Meats in Sonoma County.
